A single team order can be worth more than a month of one-off gifts. The catch is coordination: names, numbers, sizes and deadlines from a dozen people.

## Let players do the typing

Instead of collecting a spreadsheet from the coach, let each player add their name and number in the editor. Fewer typos reach production, and nobody waits on a single email thread.

## Show both sides

Numbers usually sit on the back and crests on the front. A preview of each side before checkout catches the classic mistake of a name that runs into the shoulder seam.

## Make bulk easy

- Clear size charts next to the size picker
- Quantity options that do not require a cart per player
- A deadline note so teams order in time for the season

## Reward the bigger order

Quantity pricing turns a coach comparing quotes into a coach ordering from you. Keep the tiers simple and show the saving as the quantity grows.

## Common mistakes

- **Collecting names by email.** Spelling mistakes and missing numbers are almost guaranteed. Let players enter their own details.
- **No size chart.** Team kits are often bought for children and adults at once. A clear chart next to the size picker prevents most exchanges.
- **Missing the season start.** Teams need kits before the first game. Promote early and publish a last order date.

## Make the coach's job easy

The coach is your real customer. Give them one link to share with the team, a deadline, and a summary of who has ordered. If you can offer a team page with the crest already placed and only names and numbers left to fill in, you remove most of the work. Follow up after the season with a reorder reminder: teams that were happy rarely switch suppliers, and next year's order is often bigger than this one.
