Custom products have a photo problem: you cannot photograph every possible design. The answer is clean blanks, consistent angles and a live preview that shows the shopper's own design.

## Clean blanks first

Shoot every blank on the same neutral background, with the same light. Consistent photos make a collection look professional and make each product easier to compare.

## Show it in use

One lifestyle photo per product, a mug on a desk or a hoodie on a person, helps shoppers picture the result. Keep it simple so the product, not the scene, is the focus.

## Same angles, every product

- Front, back and a detail shot
- The same crop and distance across a collection
- A size reference for small items

## Let the preview personalize

You do not need a photo of every design. A live preview on the clean blank shows each shopper their own version, which is more convincing than any sample photo.

## Common mistakes

- **Mixed backgrounds.** A white mug on grey, a tee on wood and a tote on a bed look like three different stores. Pick one background and stick to it.
- **Heavy filters.** Shoppers want to see the real colour of the blank. Keep editing light and colours true.
- **Only sample designs.** A photo full of your own artwork can make shoppers think the product only comes that way.

## A small photo setup

You do not need a studio. A large window, a white sheet of card, and a phone on a simple stand produce clean, consistent photos. Shoot at the same time of day so the light matches, and keep a note of the distance and angle for each product type. Replace a product's photos when you change suppliers, because blanks from different makers often differ in colour and cut. Consistency, more than expensive equipment, is what makes a collection look trustworthy.
