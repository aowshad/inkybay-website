A blank canvas is intimidating. Templates give shoppers a starting point, and the best ones feel like they were made for the shopper's exact occasion.

## Start from occasions

Birthdays, weddings, teams, holidays. Design templates around the reasons people buy personalized products, and name them that way.

## Edit the obvious, lock the rest

Names, dates and short messages should be editable. Layout and colours that keep the design balanced can stay locked, so the result still looks good after the shopper's changes.

## Keep the list tidy

- Group templates into categories
- Retire templates nobody uses
- Show a fresh one or two at the top each season

## Common mistakes

- **Too many choices.** Fifty similar templates slow shoppers down. A few strong options per occasion work better.
- **Locking everything.** If shoppers cannot change the colours or the message, the template feels like a finished product they do not quite want.
- **Unlocking everything.** If every element can move, a few edits can break the layout. Lock what keeps it balanced.

## Measure and refresh

Watch which templates are used and which are only viewed. Retire the ones that are never chosen and make more in the style of your bestsellers. Refresh seasonal templates every year, even if only slightly, so returning shoppers see something new. Name templates after the occasion and the style, for example "Birthday, bold" or "Wedding, classic", so shoppers can find them in a category without opening each one.

## In short

Design templates around real occasions, let shoppers edit names, dates and messages, lock what keeps the layout balanced and keep the list short and fresh. Good templates turn hesitant browsers into confident buyers, because starting from something is always easier than starting from nothing.

If you only change one thing after reading this, make it the habit you can repeat every week. Small, steady improvements to how you run a custom store add up faster than any single big change, and they are much easier for a small team to keep doing.
