This privacy policy explains how ParseLab ("ParseLab", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information in connection with InkyBay, our product customizer app for Shopify (the "App"), and the website at which this policy is published (the "Website"). Together we call these the "Services".

By using the Services you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

## 1. Who we are

InkyBay is developed and operated by ParseLab, with a head office at 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware, United States, and a development office at House 514, Road 7, Avenue 4, Mirpur DOHS, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

For questions about this policy or your personal information, email [support@inkybay.com](mailto:support@inkybay.com).

## 2. Our role: controller and processor

We handle personal information in two different roles:

- **As a controller** for information about merchants who install the App, people who contact us, newsletter subscribers and visitors to the Website. We decide why and how this information is used.
- **As a processor (service provider)** for information about shoppers who use the App on a merchant's store. The merchant is the controller of that information, and we process it only on the merchant's instructions to provide the App. If you are a shopper, the privacy policy of the store you bought from also applies, and you should contact that store first about your information.

## 3. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Contact and partner forms:** your name, email address, store or company website, the topic you choose, your message and any file you attach.
- **Newsletter:** your email address.
- **Support conversations:** the details you share when you email us or use live chat.

### Information from Shopify when a merchant installs the App

- Store details such as the store name, domain, contact name and email, country, currency and Shopify plan.
- Product and catalogue information the App needs to set up customizable products.
- Order information for customized orders, which may include shopper names, contact and shipping details, so that we can produce designs and print files for the merchant.

Shopify processes all payments for the App. We do not receive or store payment card numbers.

### Information created when shoppers use the App

- Design content: text, uploaded images and artwork, and the options and colours a shopper chooses.
- Quote requests: the shopper's name, email address, message and design, sent to the merchant.
- Print files and previews generated from these designs.

### Information collected automatically

- **Server logs:** our hosting providers record technical information such as IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request, to deliver and secure the Services.
- **Browser storage:** the Website stores small preferences in your browser, such as your chosen blog layout and whether you have already seen the 404 page hint. These stay on your device and are not used to track you.

The Website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

## 4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- provide, operate and maintain the App, including saving designs and generating print files for merchants;
- set up and manage merchant accounts and the App's billing through Shopify;
- answer questions, provide support and process partner applications;
- send our newsletter to people who have asked for it (every email has an unsubscribe link);
- keep the Services secure, prevent abuse and fix problems;
- understand how the App is used so we can improve it;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our [Terms of service](../terms-of-service/).

## 5. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases:

- **Contract:** to provide the App to merchants and to respond to requests you make.
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure, support and improve the Services, in ways that do not override your rights.
- **Consent:** for the newsletter and for any optional processing we ask you to agree to. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

## 6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share it only:

- **With service providers** who help us run the Services, such as hosting and cloud infrastructure, email delivery, form handling and customer support tools. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
- **With Shopify**, as needed to provide the App on the Shopify platform.
- **With the merchant**, for shopper information collected through the App on that merchant's store.
- **For legal reasons**, when we believe disclosure is required by law, or necessary to protect our rights, our users or the public.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to protect your information.

## 7. International transfers

We operate from the United States and Bangladesh, and our service providers may process information in other countries. When we transfer personal information from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we use appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy:

- **Merchant and store data** is kept while the App is installed. After a merchant uninstalls the App, Shopify sends us a request to erase the store's data 48 hours later, and we then delete it, except for records we must keep by law, such as billing records.
- **Shopper data** is deleted when the merchant's store data is deleted, or earlier when we receive a shopper deletion request through Shopify or from the merchant.
- **Messages and form submissions** are kept for as long as we need them to respond and to keep a record of our conversation.
- **Newsletter data** is kept until you unsubscribe.

## 9. How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and limiting access to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond quickly to any incident.

## 10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
- correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- delete your information;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- receive your information in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent;
- opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither).

To make a request, email [support@inkybay.com](mailto:support@inkybay.com). We may need to verify your identity before acting on it. We respond within the time the law requires, usually within one month (45 days for California residents). We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

**Shoppers:** because merchants control the information collected on their stores, we will pass your request to the merchant or act on the merchant's instructions. Shopify also lets merchants send us data requests on your behalf.

## 11. Children

The Services are meant for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Links to other websites

The Services link to other websites and services, such as Shopify and our help center. Their own privacy policies apply to them, and we are not responsible for their practices.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will give notice through the Website or the App. Using the Services after an update means you accept the updated policy.
